Sentiero

2023 • Encore Music

Mauro Spanò's debut as a band leader is a journey of profound inner research, a path where artistic exploration merges closely with personal experience. Divided symmetrically between four original tracks and four arrangements of works deeply dear to the author, the album features the extraordinary participation of clarinetist Gabriele Mirabassi on some tracks. The result is a sonic fresco capable of evoking, as highlighted by critics, "impressionistic ruminations and Scandinavian fascinations".

Kurt!

2021 • Velut Luna

A project conceived by singer Valentina Fin that explores and develops research dedicated to the figure of Kurt Weill, a famous German composer of the early twentieth century. The ensemble, with a chamber music feel, combines a classic string quartet with a piano and voice duo. The arrangements, curated by Mauro Spanò, rework the original scores or develop from scratch in an unprecedented contemporary key. The same atmospheres, hovering between jazz and avant-garde classical music, also live on in the original tracks written specifically for the ensemble, inspired by the poems of Weill's historical collaborator, Bertolt Brecht.

Oscura Era

2021 • Wow Records

The second recording work of RAME, written and recorded during the difficult period of the pandemic. The album inherits from the previous one the link between lyrics and literary works, but moves towards less rigid compositional structures, leaving ample room for free improvisation. The arrangements push towards territories close to Free Jazz and contemporary classical music, while the title itself draws inspiration from James Bridle's essay "New Dark Age".

Rame

2018 • Emme Record Label

The recording debut of the RAME quintet, a project born in 2016 in the classrooms of the Conservatory of Vicenza. Starting from a typically jazz matrix, founded on the search for improvisation and interplay, the album collects original compositions signed by all the members of the group. The tracks and lyrics draw deep inspiration from timeless works of Italian literature and culture. The album closes with the title track, an extemporaneous improvisation that marks the beginning of the sonic experimentation that the quintet will carry forward in the following years.
