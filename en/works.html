Sentiero

2023 • Encore Music

Mauro Spanò’s debut as a bandleader is a journey of profound introspection, a path where artistic and personal exploration are deeply intertwined. Symmetrically divided between four original tracks and four arrangements of works held closely to the author's heart, the album features the extraordinary participation of clarinetist Gabriele Mirabassi on selected tracks. The result is a sonic fresco capable of evoking, as noted by critics, "impressionistic ruminations and Scandinavian fascinations".

Kurt!

2021 • Velut Luna

A project conceived by vocalist Valentina Fin that explores and develops research dedicated to the figure of Kurt Weill, the renowned early 20th-century German composer. The ensemble, with its chamber-like essence, pairs a classic string quartet with a piano and voice duo. The arrangements, curated by Mauro Spanò, either rework the original scores or develop them entirely from scratch in an unprecedented and contemporary key. The same atmospheres, balanced between jazz and avant-garde classical music, also come alive in the original pieces written specifically for the ensemble, inspired by the poems of Weill’s historic collaborator, Bertolt Brecht.

Oscura Era

2021 • Wow Records

The second album by RAME, written and recorded during the difficult period of the pandemic. The album inherits its predecessor's connection between lyrics and literary works but moves towards less rigid compositional structures, leaving ample room for free improvisation. The arrangements push into territories close to Free Jazz and contemporary art music, while the title itself draws inspiration from James Bridle’s essay “New Dark Age”.

Rame

2018 • Emme Record Label

The recording debut of the RAME quintet, a project born in 2016 in the classrooms of the Vicenza Conservatory. Starting from a distinctly jazz matrix, founded on the pursuit of improvisation and interplay, the album brings together original compositions penned by all members of the group. The tracks and lyrics draw deep inspiration from timeless works of Italian literature and culture. The album closes with the title track, a spontaneous improvisation that marks the beginning of the sonic experimentation the quintet would carry forward in the years to follow.
